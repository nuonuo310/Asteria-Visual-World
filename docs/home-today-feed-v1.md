# Home · Today four-card visual restoration

The four persistent cards are Together, For You, Little Ones, and Brought Home, in that order. Their full-width raised-paper cards and left-hand timeline/dots match the approved visual reference. The section starts collapsed and expands on demand. Each card stays present even without an event. Empty cards display a neutral pending message, never a fabricated time or story.

A future authorized event aggregator may write `todayFeed` with a local `date` and at most three `events` using the category keys `together`, `forYou`, `littleOnes`, `broughtHome`, a real `HH:mm` time, summary `text`, and nonempty `source`. The Home view checks the date and event shape, then fills the corresponding card; it does not infer authenticity from source text and cannot aggregate real chat data by itself. It ignores legacy manual footprints without deleting them. Do not auto-generate placeholder events. Do not change the four-card layout when adding real data.
